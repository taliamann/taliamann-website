About Me

I'm a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist providing virtual therapy to adolescents and adults throughout California and Pennsylvania. I specialize in eating disorders, grief & loss and trauma.

My clinical experience includes residential treatment, PHP and IOP programs, and private practice, where I have supported individuals navigating eating disorders, substance use, trauma, OCD, grief, and anxiety. I have also completed specialized grief training through Our House Grief Support Center.

My passion for this work is deeply personal. After experiencing profound loss and recovering from a decade-long eating disorder, I understand the courage it takes to heal. These experiences shape the compassion, empathy, and understanding I bring to my work.

Above all, I believe therapy is built on a safe, trusting relationship. My goal is to provide a space where you feel seen, supported, and accepted without judgment or pressure to have it all figured out. Together, we can work toward healing, growth, and creating a life that feels aligned with who you want to be.
